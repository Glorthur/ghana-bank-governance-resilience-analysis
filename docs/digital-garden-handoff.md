# Digital Garden handoff

This document records the handoff boundary for the Digital Garden. The Garden remains unchanged in this phase; no notes, links, or claims should be edited automatically from this repository.

## Suggested handoff contents

When a curator chooses to publish a Garden note, link to the repository and cite Gloria Arthur. Describe it as a reproducibility package for a 2020–2025 Ghanaian bank panel with 95 primary observations from 16 banks. Link readers to the model and limitations documents, and preserve the distinction between disclosure evidence and realised operational resilience.

## Claims to preserve

The baseline FE association for board independence is negative and statistically significant under the planned clustered and wild-cluster inference. Firm size and the board-independence × firm-size interaction are not statistically supported. These findings are associational and constrained by the ceiling-heavy ORI and short panel.

## Data and provenance boundary

The repository begins at the frozen, manually verified CSV and documents provenance without distributing source PDFs or extracted report text. A Garden note should not imply that this repository independently reproduces annual-report retrieval or manual coding. Refer to `docs/reproducibility.md` for the complete boundary.
